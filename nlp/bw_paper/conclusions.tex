\section{Conclusions} \label{sec:conclusions}

We have presented a practical and scalable BW framework that addresses long-standing statistical and structural challenges in both vision and language models.
By extending these techniques to CBW, we enable effective whitening in autoregressive LLMs while preserving causality and computational efficiency.
Empirical results demonstrate improved optimization stability, gradient isotropy, and competitive or superior downstream performance, establishing BW and CBW as viable normalization mechanisms for large-scale deep learning.
