Batch Whitening (BW) improves optimization by decorrelating feature channels, but its adoption in large-scale models, particularly LLMs, has been limited by statistical, structural, and causal challenges.
In this work, we introduce a stable and scalable BW framework for both vision and language models.
For vision, we propose three complementary techniques -- Reorder \& Split, Controlling Estimation Error, and Handling Correlated Channels -- that improve whitening performance and reduce estimation noise.
Building on these insights, we develop Causal Batch Whitening (CBW) for autoregressive LLMs, preserving causality while maintaining computational efficiency and reliable covariance estimation.
Our approach integrates naturally with modern deep learning architectures, improving optimization stability, gradient isotropy, and downstream performance.
Extensive empirical evaluation demonstrates that BW and CBW achieve competitive or superior results compared to LayerNorm, GroupNorm, and prior whitening methods.
